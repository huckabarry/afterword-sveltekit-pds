I was raised in Michigan and grew up on Buddy’s and Jet’s pizza, but I don’t remember hearing it called “Detroit-style pizza” until my mid-30s, after I had moved to Portland. I was visiting Austin in 2015 when I saw a food cart advertising “authentic” Detroit-style slices. Now it’s everywhere.
