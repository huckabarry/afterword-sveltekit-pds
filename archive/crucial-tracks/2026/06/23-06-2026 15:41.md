*"Daughter" by Pearl Jam*

[Listen on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/daughter/1826659396?i=1826659421)

<div>I was 11 or 12 when this came out. I remember listening to this song with my uncle, who was seven years older than me and someone I completely idolized as a kid. He’d drive me around in his hatchback Mustang with the windows down, listening to this track on repeat. Since I didn't have an older brother, he was the closest thing I had to one. Without a father, my uncles were my primary male role models at that point. Shortly after, he moved to Florida and I moved to another city. A lot changed during that time for a pre-teen.
